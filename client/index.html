Flock

A shared pasture. Everyone who opens this page gets one sheep, and every sheep lives here together. They wander, graze, nap and play on their own, whether or not anyone is watching.

Your sheep grows while you're here. It earns 1 EXP for each minute this tab is open and in view. Feeding and petting are just for fun. Nothing can be bought.

Your sheep lives in this browser. There's no account. If you clear this browser's data, or open Flock somewhere else, you'll get a new lamb — the old one stays in the pasture, and after 30 days away it rests in the barn.

What we keep: your sheep, its EXP, and the country your connection came from when it was born. No name, no IP address, nothing you type.

Tap any sheep to see where its owner is. Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom.
